How to play

  • Numbers count the mines hidden in the eight surrounding cells.
  • Right-click (or long-press, or Flag mode) to mark mines.
  • Click a revealed number with all its mines flagged to open the rest of its neighbors (chording).
  • Your first click is always safe. Clear every safe cell to win.
