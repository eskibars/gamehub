Jacks or Better five-card draw: bet, hold the cards you want, and draw. A pair of jacks or better pays — a royal pays 250× the bet. Your bank survives between visits, and cashing out converts profit into hub chips.

Tap cards to hold (marked HOLD). Wins pay the multiplier × your bet.
