How it works

  1. Players take turns rolling one die, once.
  2. Roll a number the rolled-for part still needs and you mark it. If that hit completes the part, you draw it there and then.
  3. The die changes hands after every roll — a roll you can't use (that part is already drawn, or you haven't earned it yet) simply forfeits your turn.
  4. The game runs until one player has all six parts, then the gallery compares everything.

Every roll picks a part of the ladybug:

  • 1 Body
  • 2 Head
  • 3 Spots
  • 4 Wings
  • 5 Antennae
  • 6 Feet

First bug with all six parts wins, then everyone compares drawings — the weirder the better.
