Word Guess

How to play

  • Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.
  • GREEN means the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
  • GOLD means the letter is in the word but the wrong spot.
  • GRAY means the letter is not in the word at all.
  • Hard mode: revealed hints must be reused in every later guess.
  • Daily mode gives everyone the same word each day; Practice deals a fresh random word.
