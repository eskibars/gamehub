Mancala

Sow seeds counter-clockwise from any pit on your side. Landing your last seed in your store grants another turn; landing it in an empty pit on your side captures the opposite pit. Most seeds in the store wins.

Tap a glowing pit. Seeds drop one per pit going counter-clockwise (up the far side, down yours), skipping the robot's store.
